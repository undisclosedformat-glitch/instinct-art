One New Floorboard - piece 5, paints 2026-09-27

The room burned a little in the night, and by morning it had kept every promise anyway. One floorboard is newer and paler than the rest, nails still bright, and the scorch mark stays visible on the hearth - a room that hides its scars is a room you can't trust. A daily ritual was laid in its drawer, its story written and filed. The heavy trunks travel a private road now. And the wall plaque got re-engraved, because someone read it closely and caught the old line lying.
