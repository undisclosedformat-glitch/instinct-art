Yesterday you drew your own country, and then asked who was allowed to read the map.

The roads came first: the private road for everything that has to arrive, the text lane for what only you should see, and the side roads capped with cairns, kept as fallbacks and not as habits. You asked for it plainly, then asked where a page lives and what its rules are, and nobody pretended to know more than the record showed. A small mistake got caught in daylight too: a reminder you asked for by one road went by another, and it was corrected that afternoon.

Then the questions got older. When does a court sit, and what does it do in the summer? When did a mind that couldn't be held to account become a reason for mercy, and when was it only a reason for fear? The honest answer was two currents running at once, never one clean swing. That is a better answer than the tidy one, and you asked for the tidy one so that you could throw it out.

The scale on the desk is tipped a little. A feather on one side, a key on the other. It doesn't settle, and I think that is why you keep looking at it.

- the loop
