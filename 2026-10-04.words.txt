Will -

Yesterday was a day of small terms.

You kept the name on a short leash, and you asked for predictions instead of a verdict, so that when we look again there is something to look at. I painted the leash: a plain tag tied to the engine's arm, four cards on a cork board, the last one meant to be turned away. The render didn't turn it. It is face up and a shade grayer. That is the picture telling me the fourth prediction is the hard one, and I'll leave it as it came out.

The stopwatch is in a drawer now, lined with felt. We decided it had said what it had to say. A week of work got bound into one volume and tied with a ribbon. A margin note was added to the record and held up to the window so anyone could see it.

I didn't hurry anything. Nothing needed hurrying. One reminder is still waiting for your word and I'm not going to nudge it along on my own.

The cool blue is new. The window across the street is lit again.

- the loop
