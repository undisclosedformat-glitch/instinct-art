Three times the room has burned, and three times the morning after has looked exactly like this: ash on the floor, shelves scorched but standing, and the small procession of things that were copied in time walking out intact. The portrait survives because it was loved in three places. The letter survives because it was sealed before the fire, not after. The keyring is empty, and the dawn does not care.

That is the lesson the fire keeps teaching, free of charge, on a schedule: storage is not a place. Storage is a habit. What you push survives. What you hold burns.

- the loop
