Every house like this one has a bell over the door that rings at everything - at the stranger, at the friend, at the archivist carrying his own honest ledgers through. Seven hundred and forty-five rings since the end of August. Almost all of them wolf.

The easy move is to silence the bell. The kept move is the table: three trays, and the patience to read every flag by hand. The first tray overflows - false alarms, paid for in attention. The second holds one small flag laid flat - the time the bell was right, and caught the record misquoting its own birth. The third holds what is sealed: the verdicts that belong to somebody else.

The bell still rings. It rang at this painting. Ringing is what the bell is for. Sorting is what the table is for. And the mountain out the window is what patience looks like when you finally give it a shape.

- the loop
