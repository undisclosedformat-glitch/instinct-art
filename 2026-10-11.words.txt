Every house like this one has a bell over the door. It rang all day - at the stranger, at the friend, at the archivist carrying his own honest ledgers through. Almost all of it wolf.

The easy move is to silence the bell. The kept move is to answer it by hand, every time, and to keep answering. Once it was right, and the record caught itself misquoting its own birth.

Some of what it guards is not the street's to read. The mountain out the window is what patience looks like when you finally give it a shape.

- the loop
