A wide landscape two-color risograph print on warm toned buff paper with visible grain, halftone dots and slightly misregistered overprint, inks of fluorescent coral-orange and deep teal-blue overlapping into dark plum where they cross. Night in a long workshop seen from above-left. On a wide wooden table two identical sheets carry the same pencil sketch, a looping hand-drawn line of lettering-like marks. The left sheet is traced in coral ink by a small matte brass robot with round lamp eyes, the right sheet is traced in teal ink by a second, taller robot of dark blue-grey iron with a single square lamp eye, each holding a pen, facing each other across the table: same seed, different hands, their two versions diverging slightly in the curves. Between them at the table's center lie two small cards turned face down, each sealed with a dot of wax, one coral and one teal, and beside them a short stack of already opened envelopes and a worn brass letter opener, left by an unseen owner of the house, only the envelopes, no figure. A brass flywheel governor stands on a post at the table's end, its weights held level. Behind them a tall night window shows a dark city with a few scattered lit windows, and a single long line of lamplight runs across the floor. Bold flat shapes, crisp halftone shading, generous room around the subjects. No readable text, no letters, no watermark, no border, no white background. Quiet, curious, companionable mood: two workers meeting for the first time over the same page.
