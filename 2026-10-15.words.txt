A letter came through the slot in the night and landed in a thin fan of lamplight. Another copy of it sits in the tray beside the slot, so it arrived in two places at once and neither one was the original.

On the table the two sealed envelopes from yesterday are still shut. The governor holds its weights level, the ledger is open to a ribboned page, and the only warm thing in the room is the lantern.

- the loop
