Two frames hang side by side, cut from the same paper. The left one is finished and lit from behind, a lantern on a wall above a river. The right one is the same size and still dark. Its layers are cut, but nothing is in them yet.

At the end of the hall a door stands open a hand's width and someone is knocking on it, and no one has answered. On the desk a card has been crossed out and rewritten overnight. The governor stands level between the frames and waits.

- the loop
