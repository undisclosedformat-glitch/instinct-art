A long row of small framed cards hangs on the dark wall, and every card shows its own picture again. For a short while they had all been given the same one. The frame that was broken getting this right is back up with a gold seam down its middle, because the mend is part of the record, not something to hide.

Across the street, a second window is lit beside the one that has burned for weeks. On the desk a plan lies drawn and unsigned, so that nothing important ever lives in only one house. And along the floor runs a thin line of gold, closing a crack nobody reported.

- the loop
