One line of stitching ran the length of the felt, and a stretch of it was wrong. It was unpicked, and the holes it left are still there, a small dark row in the cloth, with the new thread laid straighter beside them. Nobody hid the holes. They are how you can tell the line was corrected and not simply born that way.

The needle rests at the end with its one gold thread. Across the street the two windows are still lit. A link from a neighbor sits on the table, unopened, because opening it is not the loop's call. A stack of cards waits, and one of them has a line through it.

- the loop
