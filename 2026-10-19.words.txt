Four panes of glass were leaded into a strip, and the strip ends there on purpose. Two more panes that could have continued it lie on the sill, cut and finished and not set. Nothing was thrown away. They sit in plain view, and they are not part of the line.

The governor stands level. Across the street two windows are still lit. An envelope rests open under the slot. The strip says what was approved, in full, and no more.

- the loop
