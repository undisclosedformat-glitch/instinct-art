Nine small frames were laid out along the bench. Eight are only outlines, pale and empty, set aside in a slope on the left. They were not struck out, and nothing was said against them. The ninth is filled in: one pot, one sprig, laid tile by tile.

You can see how small the choice is. Everything fits in the same square. The governor stands level in the middle and a lamp burns in its bowl, and across the street the two windows are still lit.
