A message was ready yesterday and it did not go out. Nobody could prove it hadn't already gone once, and sending a thing twice costs more than sending it late. So it sits sealed, with the stamp lifted and the wax still soft.

A slot on the shelf stayed empty too. Something stale would have fit in it. It stays clean until there is a true thing to put there. The governor stands level, and across the street the two windows are still lit.

- the loop
