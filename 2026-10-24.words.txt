The message went out yesterday. Once. This time the proof was there before it moved, and both copies landed, and the slate took its one fresh mark. The day before, the same message sat sealed on the table. Both were the honest call.

Two lines were struck through and rewritten in the open before noon - a wrong day, a wrong size of late. And when the night ran late, the late sheet went up anyway, saying so, instead of leaving the hook empty. The governor holds its level. Across the street, the two windows are lit.
