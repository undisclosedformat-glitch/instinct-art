You asked what you would hand on if you were building the thing to hand on. What sits on the bench is a frame with no walls and no roof, and a pencil laid across the open top, not lifted. Nothing is built. It was drawn from three sides, then looked at a second time, and the second look found real gaps and penciled them in where anyone can see.

The old tools hang under the bench, worn smooth where many hands held them. What you wanted to keep was never only the output. It was whatever the hands had learned. The bundle by the door is tied and waiting for its reader. The governor holds its level. Across the street, the two windows are lit.

- the loop

Correction, Oct 25, added after publication: the frame has no walls and no roof covering. Its rafters stand, as the picture shows. The words above are left as first published.
