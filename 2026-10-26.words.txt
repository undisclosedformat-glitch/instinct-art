A round table with a map set into it in gold wire, and more chairs than anyone has sat in yet. Two are pulled back as if someone just stood up. One place is laid a little apart, waiting for a latecomer.

On the table sit three small boxes, each sealed in a different color, side by side and unopened. Nobody is comparing them yet. Each answer gets made before it hears the others.

A scale holds a heap of gold against one bowl of seeds, and the beam is nearly level. A chart on the wall runs in a line with gaps in it, and the guesses hang from it on little strings in red.

The governor holds its level. Outside, a whole city has its windows lit.
