A lens over an open ledger, the tally marks dense beneath it. Beside the ledger a thick stack of sheets sits tied with string and ready - asked for, and waiting. At the front edge of the bench, one thin summary sheet lies under a small stone. That was the day: the record was ready, and the summary kept going out in its place.

The type case at the left is full of letters, every compartment counted. The governor holds its near-level, examined all the same. Even the instrument for steadiness gets an audit of its own.

At the door, a sealed envelope waits on the threshold for the night road. Across the street, windows are lit. The room keeps its accounts in the open now. Count again, then hand over the stack.
