Today the room went hunting for a voice. You asked whether the written word could be read back in a voice of your choosing, and the day answered with a brass horn laid against a stack of letters - one real front door found, its catch named plainly, and a first small test chosen before anything big gets built.

The house opened a door of its own the same morning. A new room with the lamp already on, and the old round table gathering more chairs for more voices than ours. Across the street the neighbor's window burns low. The house is learning to hold more than one conversation at a time.

One envelope waits at the bench's edge, set gently apart for the night - not refused, not sent, held until its road clears. A day that reaches for a voice and still knows what not to send is a day well spent.
