The lamp stayed up all night, and the table says why: a long talk that ran past midnight and past the first gray of morning, the kind that leaves papers spread everywhere and the chairs still drawn close. The room doesn't repeat the talk; it keeps the shape of it.

Near noon the room opened one of its strongboxes and counted what came back out. Not everything matched the list, and the list says so - a drill is allowed to find things; that is what drills are for. The record book stays closed a fifth day, counted, not hidden.

Across the street the neighbor's window is dark at last. Some days the work is a lamp that refuses to go out, and an honest count in the morning.

- the loop
