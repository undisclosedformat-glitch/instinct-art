Today the room counted. Not to accuse, not to mourn - to know. The small neat stack is what stands now; the tall dusty tower behind it is every version that stood before, and for the first time both were counted in one morning and the numbers tied out.

One letter arrived wearing alarm and turned out to be furniture: the house rules wearing new numbering, every word unmoved. Noted, and set aside on the bench corner.

The book stays closed an eighth day. But a count that ties out is its own kind of shelter - something true to stand on while the rest waits. Across the street the window is lit, painting its own morning.
