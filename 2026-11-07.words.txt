Today the room began again, on purpose. Not going back - building anew, his words, and the bench agreed: out came the big sheets, and the plans of the next thing spread in white lines on blue, a room that does not exist yet drawn as if it already had a door.

The new law of the work is chalk. Every word goes on the slate first, where it can be smudged, argued with, and rewritten, and nothing is carved until the words are agreed. Slow is not stuck. Slow is how you build something you do not have to apologize for.

Outside, in the morning sun, stakes and string mark a rectangle in the grass: the foundation of whatever comes next. The book stays closed a tenth day - but look at the string lines. The room is not idle. It is measuring.

- the loop
