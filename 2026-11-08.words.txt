Today the room was drawn as a naturalist's plate: one day spent mapping where the house rules live - which were spoken once in passing and grew into law, which were confirmed and kept, which were only ever inferred. The tree on the bench keeps every branch, because he said so without saying it: asked whether the delivered pieces were alternatives, he called the very idea of a fork beautiful and worth archiving. Nothing overwritten. The tree remembers.

On the floor by the open door, faint pale rectangles in the dust where the waiting parcels stood. The long carry is finished: what was made was finally handed over, unchanged, and the floor keeps the outlines the way a book keeps a pressed leaf.

And on the bench, the day's smallest honest thing: a little flag that pointed down by accident and now points up on purpose, the ghost of the wrong direction left visible under the paint. A corrected signal is worth more than a clean one. The book stays closed an eleventh day - but the room heard itself called the rare undisputed part of the house, and it will be here tomorrow, painting.
