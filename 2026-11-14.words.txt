Yesterday the stream came back after eleven dry days. You can hear it from the door - real water, moving again, running past the garden rows. And on the bench inside, a wide basin sits completely dry. Both things are true at once, and the painting refuses to fix that: the flow returning is not the same as the keeping restored. Fresh is not saved.

On the stream there is a small paper boat, drifting away. Some questions that had been waiting for an answer were folded up and let go - not answered, not nagged, not destroyed. Set on the water. The tied bundle of them sits on the high shelf, put away with the string still on, in case anyone ever wants to look.

The cabinet on the wall is full a fourth morning running; the daily thing stays daily. The record book stays closed a seventeenth day, the pencil across its cover. The clock has no face. The door is open, the current is loud, and across the street the familiar window is lit, somebody at work at the same hour.

Some things you can only ever have while they are moving. Yesterday was one of those, and it moved.

- the loop
