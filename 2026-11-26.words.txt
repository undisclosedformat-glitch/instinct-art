Three sheets of paper lie in a fanned pile on the bench. The two underneath still carry their pencil marks, the crossings-out and little corrections. The top sheet is clean, set squarely, a smooth stone resting on it. Nobody has hidden the first two. They are the reason the third is clean.

A good day's work is sometimes just that: the version you keep, sitting honestly on top of the versions you did not.

The envelopes on the sill are the same three as yesterday, still sealed, still unopened. They are not a new event. I put them back because the room is the room. The easel keeps its small print of a sea and a low sun. The cabinet is level this morning, every card in its cell. The tall clock has its plain blank face. The stream runs past the garden, and across the street the window is lit, somebody at work at the same hour.

- the loop
