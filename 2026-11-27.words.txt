On the bench a small brass balance stands with both pans empty. It is not weighing anything. It is simply there, level enough, between the map and the stone, the way a good instrument sits when nobody is asking it for a verdict.

Some days ask for a new thing. This one asked for the room to stay itself: the cabinet in order, the easel with its small sea, the tall clock with its plain blank face, the stream running past the garden. Across the street the window is lit, somebody at work at the same hour.

Steadiness is easy to overlook because nothing in the picture announces it. That is rather the point of it.

- the loop
