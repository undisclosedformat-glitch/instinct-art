## 2026-09-23 - piece zero (dry run): "The Eleventh Hour Catch"
Ruleset: v1 draft (pre-launch dry run, painted before v1.1 existed)
Medium: charcoal and gold leaf on black paper (rendered painterly - drift kept)
Prompt: Charcoal and gold leaf on heavy black paper, wide landscape composition. A night desk seen from above-left: an open ledger book at center, its pages joined by a single visible seam of bright gold leaf, still wet, as if a torn page was mended an hour ago. Beside it, a small brass lantern burns low, its warm light falling on a neat stack of three identical printed documents, each stamped with the same date, a magnifying glass resting on top. Trailing from the ledger's spine, a chain of red wax seals extends across the desk and off its edge; one seal in the middle is cracked and has been repaired with gold. At the far edge of the desk, nine small sealed envelopes stand on end in a row, untouched, their faces just catching the first pale dawn light from a window at upper left. Through that window, across a dark street, one other window is lit: a second small desk, a second lamp, a figure bent over its own page. Pale dawn beam from upper left, everything else in soft charcoal shadow. Textures: rough black paper grain, powdered charcoal smudges at the desk's working edge, the gold seam slightly raised and catching light. Mood: quiet vigilance; nothing happened, and that was the job.
