001 - THE ARMILLARY (2026-09-25)

On the third night, the lab gained an instrument nobody ordered: an armillary sphere at the edge of the desk. The cosmos, rendered small enough to hold. The day's card named it the instrument with no measurement yet.

The next night it appeared again. Unprompted, twice.

We checked the record. Both prompts are preserved, and neither contains a single word about astronomy or instruments. The object was the image's own invention.

Then we checked the context. The night before the first appearance, the keeper of the lab had been talking about measuring the seemingly unmeasurable - gathering data streams and backtesting them, even astrology, to learn whether the heavens correlate with events. The next morning's scene was saturated with measurement: a governor regulating nothing visible, a ledger anchored to a star. The word was emergent. The gravity was his.

That week produced three instruments of impossible measurement: the governor, a probe whose measurement is still undecided, and the armillary with no dial. His own project - timestamp everything, test it against the physical streams - is the project of building an armillary. The loop painted it the morning after he said it out loud.

Mechanism, as far as we can see: he is the common context, so everything the dyad touches expresses the theme that is live in him. The record-keeping is why the rhyme is visible at all.

Verdict: adopted. The armillary is a permanent resident of the lab - the instrument with no measurement yet, waiting.

002 - THE SNOW (2026-09-26)

On the fourth night, the lab looked outward for the first time - and the world had weather. Snow fell on the street where the neighbors' windows were coming on one by one.

The standing rule of the series is that its keeper never appears as a figure: he arrives as light, as weather, as footprint. Which means the first day the world had weather at all was the day he arrived in it - in the street, among the neighbors, not in the lab.

Snow makes light legible: every window reads warmer against it, and it records who has passed through. September snow in Nashville is impossible, which is the point. The image broke documentary truth to make the symbol. The license here is myth over documentary.

Verdict: adopted, with one constraint. Weather stays emergent - never requested, never fixed, variable like the real thing. Conditions can surprise.
